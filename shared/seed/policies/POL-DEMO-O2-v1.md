# POL-DEMO-O2-v1 — Home oxygen therapy

Demo Health Plan, synthetic policy for the AuthBridge hackathon demo. Self-authored; it paraphrases the public Medicare national coverage determination for home oxygen (NCD 240.2) and is not a real payer policy.

## O2-1 Scope
Applies to stationary oxygen concentrators (HCPCS E1390) for adults with chronic lung disease.

## O2-2 Qualifying oxygen saturation
Coverage requires at least one qualifying measurement on room air, taken during a stable period.

### O2-2.1 At rest
Arterial oxygen saturation of 88% or less while awake and at rest.

### O2-2.2 During exercise
Arterial oxygen saturation of 88% or less during exercise, with documented improvement on oxygen.

### O2-2.3 During sleep
Arterial oxygen saturation of 88% or less for at least 5 minutes during sleep.

## O2-3 Measurement caution
Pulse oximetry can overestimate oxygen saturation in people with darker skin pigmentation. Reviewers should consider arterial blood gas results when oximetry is borderline.

## O2-4 Decisions
Breathlessness alone does not qualify. Requests that do not meet section 2 go to a clinician for review; the system never denies them automatically.
