# POL-DEMO-GLP1-v1 — GLP-1 receptor agonist for chronic weight management (continuation)

Demo Health Plan, synthetic policy for the AuthBridge hackathon demo. Self-authored and inspired by the general shape of public plan criteria; it is not a real payer policy. Pharmacy-benefit drugs are outside CMS-0057-F; this scenario is forward-looking.

## GLP-1 Scope
Applies to continuation of semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly after the initial authorization period.

## GLP-2 Continuation criteria
All of the following must be met.

### GLP-2.1 Time at maintenance dose
The patient has completed at least 12 weeks at the maintenance dose.

### GLP-2.2 Response to therapy
The patient has lost at least 5% of baseline body weight.

## GLP-3 Decisions
Requests that do not meet section 2 go to a clinician for review.
