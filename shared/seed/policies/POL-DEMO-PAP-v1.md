# POL-DEMO-PAP-v1 — CPAP for obstructive sleep apnea (initial 12 weeks)

Demo Health Plan, synthetic policy for the AuthBridge hackathon demo. Self-authored; it paraphrases the structure of public Medicare criteria (LCD L33718, Positive Airway Pressure Devices) and is not a real payer policy.

## PAP-1 Scope
Applies to initial coverage of a CPAP device (HCPCS E0601) for adults with obstructive sleep apnea.

## PAP-2 Coverage criteria
Coverage requires all of section 2.1 and either section 2.2 or section 2.3.

### PAP-2.1 Face-to-face evaluation
The treating practitioner documented an in-person clinical evaluation for sleep apnea before the sleep test was performed.

### PAP-2.2 Sleep test with AHI of 15 or more
A covered sleep test shows an apnea-hypopnea index (AHI) or respiratory disturbance index of at least 15 events per hour.

### PAP-2.3 Sleep test with AHI of 5 to 14 plus a qualifying condition
The AHI is between 5 and 14 events per hour and the record documents excessive daytime sleepiness, impaired cognition, mood disorders, insomnia, hypertension, ischemic heart disease or history of stroke.

## PAP-3 Documentation
Submit the face-to-face note and the sleep test report. Requests missing either document are pended, not denied.
