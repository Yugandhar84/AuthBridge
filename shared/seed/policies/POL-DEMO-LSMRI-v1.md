# POL-DEMO-LSMRI-v1 — MRI of the lumbar spine without contrast

Demo Health Plan, synthetic policy for the AuthBridge hackathon demo. Self-authored; it paraphrases commonly published imaging criteria and is not a real payer or vendor guideline.

## MRI-1 Scope
Applies to CPT 72148 for low back pain with or without radiculopathy in adults.

## MRI-2 Gold card
Ordering practitioners with a gold-card status for this service do not need prior authorization.

## MRI-3 Medical necessity
Either section 3.1 or section 3.2 must be met.

### MRI-3.1 Red-flag findings
Imaging is appropriate without a trial of conservative care when the record documents cauda equina signs (saddle anesthesia, new bladder or bowel dysfunction), progressive or severe neurological deficit, suspected cancer, suspected infection, or significant trauma.

### MRI-3.2 Failed conservative care
Symptoms persist after at least 6 weeks of conservative care, such as anti-inflammatory medication, physical therapy, a home exercise program or activity modification.

## MRI-4 Documentation
When conservative care is under 6 weeks and no red flag is documented, the request is pended with a request for physical therapy notes or other records of conservative care.
